# Anchor

## Register

product

## Users

Anchor is primarily evaluated by recruiters, engineering managers, and senior AI engineers. They arrive with limited time and need to understand the system's scope, run a representative regulatory question, inspect the grounded answer, and verify the supporting sources without learning a novel interface.

## Product Purpose

Anchor demonstrates disciplined retrieval-augmented generation over a fixed corpus of official RBI Master Directions and SEBI Master Circulars. The public interface must make the core contract obvious: it supports a source-grounded regulatory conversation, carries recent context into follow-up questions, returns evidence when support is strong, and gives a clear refusal when it is not. Success means a reviewer can start a conversation, ask a follow-up, and verify the supporting sources within minutes.

## Brand Personality

Disciplined, credible, and transparent. The interface should feel like a focused research instrument: calm enough for careful reading, concise enough for quick evaluation, and explicit about evidence and system boundaries.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-page composition, oversized editorial headlines, decorative glass panels, warm lifestyle palettes, vague AI-assistant language, ornamental motion, and card-heavy layouts. Do not obscure citations, refusal reasons, corpus scope, or legal disclaimers behind secondary interactions.

## Design Principles

- Put the conversation and composer in the first viewport.
- Preserve recent conversations locally so users can return to prior research without requiring an account.
- Make evidence as easy to inspect as the generated answer.
- Communicate corpus boundaries and refusals with direct, non-alarming language.
- Reveal engineering rigor through useful metadata, not promotional copy.
- Prefer familiar, accessible product controls over novel interaction patterns.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.x AA contrast and interaction behavior. Preserve visible labels, keyboard navigation, strong focus states, non-color status cues, comfortable pointer targets, reduced-motion support, and robust layouts at narrow mobile widths and increased text sizes.
